My Favorite City

My Favorite City is Punto Fijo.

Punto Fijo is a coastal city on the Paraguaná Peninsula, famous for its strong winds and refreshing breezes.

Surrounded by beautiful beaches just minutes away, it offers both relaxation and adventure by the sea.

Beyond its natural charm, it is my birthplace, which makes it deeply meaningful to me.

For all these reasons—the wind, the beaches, and the personal connection—Punto Fijo is my favorite city.
